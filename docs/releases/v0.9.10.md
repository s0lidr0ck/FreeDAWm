## What's new in 0.9.10

Five new plugins, and three of Vint's circuits rebuilt.

All five are in the insert picker and the plugin browser.

### CopyQ

A match equaliser. It measures a sound as a profile, then makes another sound's tone meet it.

- **Capture** a sound by playing it through CopyQ and saving the result, or measure an audio
  file or the edit selection directly. Silence and bleed are left out.
- **Match** by picking a profile from the library. Up to four can be blended, each with its own
  weight.
- **Amount** runs from -100 % (away from the target, to make room for it) to 200 %. **Detail**
  sets how fine the curve is and **Max Gain** how far any frequency may move.
- **Four ranges** each have their own amount; drag the display's three splits and its two edges.
- **Stereo, Mid/Side or Left/Right**, so the width carries over with the tone.
- **Linear phase**, or **minimum phase** with no latency for playing through.
- **Dynamics** follows how the target's tone changes between its quiet and loud passages.
- **Freeze** holds the match still. **Delta** plays only what it changes.
- **Link groups** make several tracks match one another: Send, Match and Group.
- **Send to CleanQ** turns the match into ordinary bands you can edit.
- Twelve presets.

CopyQ copies tonal balance and nothing else: not compression, saturation or the room. Its library
starts with five straight spectral slopes; profiles of real sounds are yours to capture. It is
new and has not been tuned by ear yet: Dynamics and its timing are first values.

### Sift

A de-esser for vocals that also catches plosives. It sorts a voice into the voice itself, its
sibilance and its plosives, and treats each on its own.

- **Sibilance** and **Plosives** each have an amount and a range, and the frequencies they
  work between.
- **Listen** plays one part on its own, to hear what is being taken off.
- Eight presets. About 8 ms of latency, which FreeDAWm compensates for.

### Lull

A gate and expander for getting one source out of a microphone that hears several.

- **Expand** turns the sound down gently below the threshold; **Gate** switches it, with Attack,
  Hold and Release.
- The detector has its own high-pass and low-pass, **Hysteresis** so it does not chatter, and
  optional **Lookahead**.
- A sidechain can **Trigger** the gate, or **Guard** it: while the thing you do not want is loud,
  the threshold rises so its bleed cannot open the microphone.

### Sprawl

A four-band stereo imager.

- Each band has its own **Width**, from mono to twice as wide, with an overall Width on top.
- What it adds disappears in mono: the mono sum of the output is the mono sum of the input.
- **Stereoize** gives a mono source some width. **Mono Safe** holds a band back when widening
  it would make it cancel in mono.
- No latency.

### Imprint

Captures the impulse response of whatever sits between two points of the signal path: another
plugin, outboard gear, or a room through a speaker and a microphone. It plays a sine sweep,
records what comes back and works out the response, ready to save as a file for an impulse
loader or a convolution reverb.

### Vint

The Maximizer, the Vitalizer and Air were rebuilt from the documents of the hardware they are
modelled on, each with a faceplate of its own in the editor. Sessions that use them may sound
different from 0.9.9.

Everything from 0.9.9 (Manners and Rez) and earlier is included.
