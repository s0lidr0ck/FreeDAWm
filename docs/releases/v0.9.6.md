## What's new in 0.9.6

Three things people asked for after a few days with Juke.

- **Juke and Lift windows can leave the main window.** Every plugin window has a **Detach**
  button over its contents: it becomes a window of its own, to put anywhere, on another screen
  too. **Dock** brings it back. Where you leave a detached window is remembered per plugin, and
  a detached window closed comes back detached.
- **Clicking an instrument opens it.** The Mix strip's INSTRUMENT slot and the Edit window's
  instrument field open the instrument itself (Juke, Lift, a plugin's own editor). The list of
  every instrument is on right-click, or on click while the track has no instrument yet.
- **Patterns are edited in one place**, the Juke window's Steps view. The MIDI Editor shows a
  pattern clip with **Open in Juke** and **Convert to MIDI** instead of a second step grid, and a
  double-click on a pattern clip in the Edit window opens the Steps view on it.
- **Duplicate** in the Steps view copies the pattern you are on right after itself, with the same
  steps, to change for the next part of the song (bring the hi-hat in, drop the kick…).

Everything from 0.9.5 (the Windows ASIO fix) and 0.9.4 (Juke, Lift, Musical Typing, MP3/Opus
export) is included.
