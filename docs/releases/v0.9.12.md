## What's new in 0.9.12

Two keys on the master fader that change what you hear and never what you bounce: **EAR**, a
hearing correction, and **SIM**, your mix through someone else's speakers.

Both sit on the first master fader in the Mix window, where other strips have Solo and Mute, and
under **Options › Monitoring**. Click to turn one on or off; right-click to choose its profile.

### Never in a bounce

EAR and SIM work on the sound on its way to your audio device and nowhere else. A bounce, an
export, a freeze or a commit is the same file whether they are on or off, and the meters show the
mix as it is, not as you hear it. Which keys are on is this computer's setting: it is not saved in
the session and is not an edit.

### SIM: hear the mix on other systems

Plays the mix through a measured output: phones, earbuds, a laptop, TVs, a car, a small radio, a
Bluetooth speaker, PA speakers and several studio headphones and monitors. **Mono** is there too.

- Each profile is the output's frequency response and how wide its stereo is. What a device does
  to loud passages (limiting, distortion) is not modelled.
- Profiles are turned down so that nothing is ever boosted above the mix: SIM cannot clip your
  output, and some profiles are quieter than the mix.
- To add your own: `freedawm-cli monitor-test-file test.wav` writes a test file; bounce it through
  a device (or a plugin that models one) and run `freedawm-cli monitor-profile bounce.wav
  --library <your Library folder>`.

### EAR: a correction for your hearing

For headphones. One curve per ear, so the mix reaches you the way it reaches someone with typical
hearing.

- **Options › Monitoring › Hearing Test…** makes the profile in about five minutes, with no special
  equipment: set a listening level, move ten bands of noise until each sits in the middle of your
  head, then make each band as loud as a reference. Use the headphones you mix on, in a quiet room.
- The result opens in **Ear Profile…**, where every band has a slider per ear. Change anything that
  sounds wrong, then Save.
- The correction only ever turns things down, so it cannot clip: turn your headphones up to taste.
- The test's sounds are not affected by EAR or SIM, so you can run it again with a profile on.

Good to know: the difference between your ears is measured directly. The balance between low and
high frequencies is an estimate, because the test cannot tell your hearing from your headphones'
own sound. This is not a medical hearing test.

### Where profiles live

In your Library folder, next to your presets and templates, under `Monitor Profiles`. If you sync
that folder between computers, your profiles come along. Which profile is in use is set per
computer, since it depends on the headphones plugged in.

### Not yet

- The keys are on the Mix window's master strip; from the Edit window, use the Options menu.
- Sample and hit previews in the browser are not passed through EAR or SIM.
- A surround main mix is passed through unchanged.
- The Hearing Test asks each question once: there is no repeat to check an answer.

Everything from 0.9.11 (Mend, Vault, the Built-In plugin list) and earlier is included.
