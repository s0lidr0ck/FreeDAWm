## What's new in 0.9.9

Two new plugins that listen to the sound and correct it as it plays: Manners balances a mix,
Rez takes out what sticks out.

Both are in the insert picker's first group and in the plugin browser, and both have a full
editor and a slim one.

### Manners

An automatic balance equaliser. Many times a second it works out which parts of the sound are
covering up their neighbours and which are being covered, and evens them out.

- **Tame** turns down what dominates. **Recover** lifts what is buried. Both go to 200 %.
- **Bias** leans the work towards one or the other. **Brighten** tilts the result brighter or
  darker, and **Boost** trades bass against the low mids.
- **The display** shows the sound going in and coming out, with the gain Manners is applying
  drawn over it: lifts above the line, cuts below. Drag the display's two edges to choose the
  range it works in; a double-click on an edge opens it again.
- **Delta** plays only what Manners is changing.
- A held tone or a whistle gets a deeper, narrower cut of its own, without turning the rest up.
- Seven presets to start from.

Manners is meant for busses and masters, in small amounts. It adds about 43 ms of latency, which
FreeDAWm compensates for. Its presets are first drafts.

### Rez

A dynamic resonance suppressor. It finds the frequencies that stick out of the sound's own
spectrum (harshness, ringing, boxiness) and turns down only those, only while they stick out.

- **Depth** sets how much it does, **Sharpness** how narrow its cuts are and **Selectivity** how
  wide a stretch of the sound each frequency is measured against.
- **Soft** and **Hard** modes, for a light touch or a firm one.
- **Four sensitivity bands** make it work harder or easier in parts of the range, between a
  **Low** and a **High** limit.
- **L/R or M/S**, with **Link** and **Balance** between the two sides, and a sidechain so
  another track can decide what gets turned down.
- **Delta** plays only what Rez is removing.
- 111 presets, sorted by the job at hand.

Everything from 0.9.8 (the Library, Generate MIDI from Text and Generate Song, hearing your clips
through a punch's pre- and post-roll) and earlier is included.
