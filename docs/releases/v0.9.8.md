## What's new in 0.9.8

A Library for what you save, two new AI tools, and a better punch-in.

### The Library

What you save to use again now lives in one folder and one place in the app: the plugin browser's
new **Library** tab.

- **Plug-in chains.** Track › **Save Plug-in Chain…** saves a track's inserts, third-party
  plug-in settings included. **Load Plug-in Chain…** puts them on other tracks, in place of
  their inserts or after them.
- **Presets that work in any session.** Chains and track presets now carry the samples their
  instruments play (the Sampler's sample, Juke's pads), and a track preset finds its send busses
  by name, making one that is missing.
- **One list.** Plug-in chains, track presets, plugin presets, Juke presets and session templates,
  with a search and a filter for each kind. Double-click one to use it on the selected track; a
  template starts a new session from it. **Save…** adds the selected track's chain or strip, or
  the session as a template.
- **Keep it where you like.** **Folder…** at the bottom of the tab moves the Library, into a
  folder a sync service keeps, say, so it is the same on every computer. Nothing already saved
  is moved.

The Library is in the desktop app.

### Generate MIDI from Text and Generate Song

Two new tools in the **AI** menu, desktop only, each one undo step.

- **Generate MIDI from Text…** writes a piece from a description: one instrument track per
  instrument at the edit cursor, with a length, a randomness and a seed to get the same piece
  again. **Continue from the clip** starts from a selected MIDI clip's notes.
- **Generate Song…** makes a whole song with vocals on a new audio track, with **YuE2** or
  **ACE-Step 1.5**. With YuE2 the score it sang also lands as MIDI tracks.
- The models and song engines install from **Setup › ML Models** (the song engines are about
  4.4 GB each). FreeDAWm starts and stops the engine itself; you can also point it at an engine
  running on another computer.

### Punching in

Recording into a selection with Pre/Post-Roll now plays an armed track's clips through the
pre-roll and the post-roll, so you hear what you are playing along to, and switches to the
input for the punch itself.

Everything from 0.9.7 (kits from your own recordings, key variants) and earlier is included.
