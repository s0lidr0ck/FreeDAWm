## What's new in 0.9.5

A fix for Windows users on ASIO.

- **Opening Setup › Playback Engine crashed on Windows with an ASIO device running** (an access
  violation, so nothing reached the log). Listing the audio systems made a second ASIO host,
  which re-initialised the running driver and then tore it down under the audio thread. With a
  device open, the ASIO drivers are now listed from the registry without loading any of them:
  the running driver shows its live channels and rate, the others their names (their details
  show once chosen). Changing the device also closes the old one before opening the new.

Everything from 0.9.4 is included; see its notes for what Juke, Lift, Musical Typing and the
MP3/Opus export bring.
