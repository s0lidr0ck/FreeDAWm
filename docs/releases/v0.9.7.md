## What's new in 0.9.7

Your own drums in Juke, and more out of each pad.

### Kits from your own recordings

- **Drop drum recordings on a pad** (or the pad menu's **Import Takes…**, or select the hits on
  an audio track and click **From Selection**, as in Lift). Every hit is found and trimmed to its
  attack and its ring, so it plays on the beat, and measured for how hard it was played.
- **The hit picker** shows them all as tiles in four colour bands, Ghost to Hard. The best sixteen
  are ticked for you, four per velocity layer. Click a tile to hear it, tick the ones you want,
  drag one to another layer, then **Apply**: the pad is built and levelled with the kit, as one
  undo step. On a pitched drum (an 808 line, say), hits at other notes are marked so they don't
  end up as wrong notes among the round-robins.
- **Build a whole kit at once**: drop a folder (or several files) anywhere else in the Juke window,
  or Kits › **Import Kit Folder…**. Each file goes to its pad by name (kick, snare, rim, clap,
  hats, toms, crash, ride, tambourine, cowbell, shaker, clave), sub-folder names count too.

### Key variants

As in OneShot 2: a pad can play on more keys, each with its own **tune, fine, level, pan, sends,
decay and cutoff**, the samples always the pad's. Right-click a pad's key in the honeycomb,
**Copy**, right-click a free key, **Paste**. The variant's key shows darker with a dot and opens
in the Pad view with its eight knobs. Up to sixteen per kit, without using up any of the 16 pads.
Pasting onto another pad's key copies the whole pad over it instead.

### The keys light up

The honeycomb along the bottom lights each key as its note plays: the track's clips and patterns,
Musical Typing, live MIDI and auditions.

Everything from 0.9.6 (detachable Juke and Lift windows, click an instrument to open it) and
earlier is included.
