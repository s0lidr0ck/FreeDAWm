## What's new in 0.11.0

Vocals, lyrics and working from another room. Tuning sounds like the singer again, there is
real-time pitch correction, typed lyrics can be timed word by word and single words given effects,
a studio's mixer can be run from another computer, and a small recorder program is installed
beside FreeDAWm on Windows.

Much of this has been tried on one Windows computer and nowhere else, and some of it only by
measurement. Keep a copy of any session that matters before opening it here, and please report
what you find.

### Changes you will notice

- **Vocal tuning keeps the singer's own sound.** Moved notes used to come out thin, phasey and a
  little robotic, and a moved note that ran straight into an unmoved one hiccuped in pitch. Both
  are fixed, stereo vocals keep their image, and moving many notes at once no longer warbles.
  Tunings you already made sound better as they are.
- **A clip that plays through an ARA plug-in says so in the Vocal Editor** and cannot be tuned
  there until the plug-in is bypassed (Clip › ARA Plug-ins...). Before, its notes could be dragged
  and nothing changed in the sound. Make Harmony now copies takes without their ARA plug-in, so
  the harmony is heard.
- **In the Vocal Editor, Ctrl+wheel up now zooms in** (it zoomed out), as in the MIDI Editor and
  the Edit window.
- **Capture to Pantry plays each note as written.** A global transpose on the Key ruler, or a
  track's MIDI Real-Time Properties, no longer shifts what is sampled, so a captured instrument
  plays in the right key.
- **Searching for a plug-in also matches what it is**, not only its name: "compressor" finds Comp,
  "pitch correction" finds Plumb.

### Vocals

- **Plumb**, real-time pitch correction for one voice (Insert › Plumb). It pulls the voice to the
  notes of the song's key from the Key ruler, key changes included, or a key and notes of its own.
  One switch chooses **Natural** (gentle, keeps the vibrato) or **Hard tune** (every note snaps),
  and can be automated. **Studio** mode is the cleanest and is lined up by the mixer; **Live** mode
  adds a few milliseconds, for singing through it, and stays in with Low Latency Monitoring on.
  Retune speed, Humanize, Keep vibrato, Note hold, Formant and Mix, with a display of the sung and
  the corrected pitch. One voice only. See `docs/plumb.md`.
- **The Vocal Editor's notes as MIDI:** its new **MIDI** menu makes an instrument track under the
  vocal with one note per sung note (your splits, joins and moves included), or writes them to a
  .mid file. The words go with them as lyrics when the vocal has timed words.
- **Scroll bars in the Vocal Editor**, for time under the notes and for pitch at the right.

### Lyrics

- **Typed lyrics can be timed to the vocal word by word, with no speech model.**
  **Timing › Time Words to Vocal** in the Lyrics tab makes a first guess from the notes and
  breaths in each line. **Timing › Tap Words...** is the dependable way: play the song and press J
  on each word. Words appear in a lane in the Vocal Editor and under the track, where you drag
  their edges, split, merge and retype them; guessed words are marked until you check them, and
  the word being sung lights up. Transcribe Lyrics asks before replacing words timed by hand. See
  `docs/lyrics.md`.
- **Gild: effects on chosen words.** In the Vocal Editor, click **Gild**, select words (or "Last
  of lines", "First of lines", every place a word is sung) and give just them a delay or reverb
  throw, a reverse swell into the word, a double, a harmony in the song's key, a pitch and formant
  shift, or grit. What Gild adds is mixed into the vocal track, with a **Dry** switch to hear only
  the additions. Copy and paste between words; presets included. See `docs/gild.md`.

### Working from another room

- **Mix a studio from another computer:** **File › Connect to a Studio...** opens that studio's
  mixer in a Remote Mix window, on the same network or over the internet with an invite. You hear
  the studio's main mix and move its faders, pans, mutes, solos, sends and built-in effects, recall
  and store mix snapshots, and undo there. Each move is one undo step at the studio, and a dropped
  connection changes nothing in its mix. The studio can **Lock remote mixing** or send a remote
  away (Setup › Network...). Third-party plug-ins show by name and bypass only. Both computers
  need 0.11.0. See `docs/remote-mixing.md`.
- **A booth PIN** (Setup › Network...): set one PIN of six characters or more and computers on
  your network pair by typing it, with nobody at the booth to read out a code. Wrong guesses pause
  it and, after twenty, turn it off; every pairing is shown on the booth. A paired computer can do
  everything, so share the PIN accordingly.
- **Reusable invites** for the internet: one invite can let several people join, for an hour, a
  day or a week, and can be revoked. Someone who has joined before reconnects without a new one.
- **The mix stream keeps playing while the booth is stopped**, so a remote hears live inputs
  between takes; a paired computer can connect only to listen; and the booth's computer is kept
  awake while remotes are connected.
- **FreeDAWm Recorder** (Windows): a small program installed with FreeDAWm, with its own Start
  menu entry. It opens on one Record button for catching an idea: a WAV, and a MIDI file when a
  keyboard was played, named by date and time in Documents\FreeDAWm Ideas. **Open in FreeDAWm**
  puts a take in a new session. It can also join a studio by its code, PIN or invite to record
  along with it and hear its mix. It never listens on the network. See `docs/recorder.md`.

### The Mix window and windows

- **Close Plug-ins**, beside the channel width buttons (also Window › Layout, Ctrl+Alt+W), closes
  every plug-in and effect window at once.
- **Colour strips** (Off, Subtle, Medium, Strong) tints each channel strip with its track colour,
  with labels and meters kept readable in both themes. Off by default.
- **Window › Full Screen** (Shift+F11; Ctrl+Cmd+F on macOS) fills the screen with the main window.
  Plain F11 remains the talkback key.

### Files and playback

- **File › Import › AAF...** brings in the tracks and clips of an AAF session from Pro Tools and
  other programs.
- **Long sessions use far less memory.** Waveform overviews are read in pieces instead of whole,
  and playing audio from disk was made more dependable; the status bar says "Disk too slow" when a
  drive cannot keep up.
- **The Band repeats itself less:** each player now varies their part from phrase to phrase under
  **As a band**.

### MIDI

- **Setup › MIDI Input Devices...** shows, under each open input, how many messages it has sent
  and the last one in words. **Rescan** now closes and reopens every input, an input that stops
  sending its keep-alive is reopened by itself, and a port another program holds says so.

### Not yet

- Gild cannot save your own presets, and has no control on the Mix strip. On a clip that plays
  through an ARA plug-in only its delay, reverb and swell work.
- Plumb in Live mode flattens only about half of a vibrato on Hard tune, and its Formant control
  is for Studio mode. Fast runs are left much as sung at Natural: no real-time corrector can see
  where a run is going.
- Remote mixing hears the main mix only, not a bus of your choice, and shows third-party plug-ins
  without their settings. FreeDAWm Recorder always records along when it has joined a studio.
- Time Words to Vocal can place a word half a second off where one word runs into the next with
  no consonant or breath between them: check the guessed words, or tap them in.
- Recording the sound of other programs (a browser, a call) is designed but not built.

### How this was checked

The automated tests pass. The vocal tuning fixes and Plumb were listened to on a real vocal, as
rendered files; the tuning, Pantry and MIDI input fixes came from sessions on Windows. The rest
(Gild, word timing, remote mixing, the Recorder's new window, Close Plug-ins, Colour strips, Full
Screen) was measured and tested but not yet tried on real sessions. Nothing here was tried on
macOS or Linux hardware, over the public internet between two homes, or with a real ARA plug-in.

Everything from 0.10.0 and earlier is included.
