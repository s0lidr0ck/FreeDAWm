## What's new in 0.9.11

Two new plugins, Mend and Vault, and plugin lists that say what each plugin does.

Both plugins are in the insert picker's first group and in the plugin browser.

### Mend

A vocal cleaner on the track, with two modes.

- **Studio** takes the room, the noise and the clipping off a voice. It uses the same model as
  Clean Vocal (Smule Renaissance, a 41 MB download that Mend's drawer offers). The model has to
  hear ahead, so Studio has about 2.9 seconds of latency. FreeDAWm makes up for it on playback,
  but you cannot monitor through it: it is for mixing.
- **Live** turns noise down by up to 18 dB with about 60 ms of latency, and needs no download. It
  leaves the room in, and it was made for speech, so held sung notes can waver. Live runs at 44.1
  and 48 kHz.
- **Amount** blends the cleaned sound with the original. **Listen** plays the cleaned sound or
  only what was taken away.
- A bounce always waits for the cleaned sound. While playing, if the computer cannot keep up, Mend
  plays the original for that stretch instead of glitching, and its drawer says so.

Studio is heavy: expect one Mend on a vocal track to be comfortable and several to strain a
computer. Mend needs the desktop app; in the browser it passes the sound through. It is new and
has not been tuned by ear across many voices yet.

### Vault

A convolution reverb. It plays a sound through the impulse response of a real place or device: a
hall, a plate, a spring, or anything captured with Imprint.

- **Load** a WAV, AIFF or FLAC from the drawer: mono, stereo, or four channels for true stereo.
  Until you load one, Vault plays a hall of its own.
- **Pre-delay**, **Decay**, **Low Cut**, **High Cut**, **Width**, **Mix** and **Output**, all of
  which work while it plays.
- No latency.

### Plugin lists

- The insert picker's first group is now called **Built-In** (it was FireStation).
- Every built-in plugin shows what it is after its name when you pick it and in its drawer:
  "Swale - Volume Swells", "Shale - Drum Gate", "Vint - Vintage EQ & Enhancers". Insert slots keep
  the short name.
- **Drum Synth** is no longer offered in the lists: Juke is the drum instrument. Sessions that use
  Drum Synth still open and play.

Everything from 0.9.10 (CopyQ, Sift, Lull, Sprawl, Imprint and the rebuilt Vint circuits) and
earlier is included.
