## What's new in 0.9.4

0.9.3 was built but never published, so this is the first release with everything from both
rounds.

### Juke, a drum machine

**Track › New Beat** gives you an instrument track playing Juke with a pattern clip, ready to go.

- **16 pads**, each a synth voice (kick, snare, clap, hats, toms, cymbals, percussion), a sample,
  or both blended. Four velocity layers × four round-robin takes per pad; drop files on the cells
  (several files fill a row). Per pad: tune, amp/pitch/filter envelopes, drive, level, pan, two
  effect sends, choke groups, stacks, reverse, one-shot or gate, and its own humanize (level,
  pitch, filter, timing).
- **The step sequencer, in the Juke window** (Steps, beside Kit and Pad): the track's pattern
  clips as tabs, steps per bar from 8 to 32, per-lane length for polymeters, rate and direction,
  shuffle, accent, humanize and fills. Click or paint steps, drag for velocity, keys 1–4 for
  Full/75/50/25. Right-click a step for probability, nudge, rolls, accent, flam and when it plays
  (every 2nd pass, first pass only, fill bars…). Convert a pattern to a plain MIDI clip whenever
  you want to edit notes by hand.
- **Two effects buses and the output**, four slots each, hosting FireStation effects; a filled
  slot opens the effect's full editor.
- **27 kits built in**: five starter kits and one for each of 21 styles (Alternative, Soft,
  Hard, Metal, '50s, '60s and Rockabilly rock; Pop, Folk, Country; Praise, Worship, Gospel/Shout;
  Boom Bap, Trap, Lo-fi; House, Techno, Drum & Bass; Reggaeton; Funk), with **48 starter grooves**
  in the Grooves menu.
- **Recorded kits**: Kits › Recorded & saved downloads **SM Drums**, **SM Drums 17** and
  **SM Drums Hybrid**, three kits of real drums (16 pads × 4 layers × 4 takes each, 180–216 MB)
  built from the SM Drums recordings by Scott McLean, Tod Stillwell and Suleiman Ali (public
  domain; SFZ programming by DSmolken). Each loads as recorded or with one of eight
  **characters** (Tight, Punch, Big Room, Vintage, Lo-fi, Tuned Up, Tuned Down, Loose).
- **101 presets** in the Presets menu, each a whole sound: the kit, every pad's settings, and
  the bus effects with their settings, in seven categories (Acoustic on the recorded kits, Rock,
  Pop & Folk, Church, Hip-Hop, Electronic, Drum Machines). Every preset was rendered, measured
  and levelled to one loudness. **Save preset** keeps your own; **Randomize** gives a pad or the
  kit a new take; Kits › Export Kit Folder writes a kit to share.

### Lift, a sampler

An instrument that plays one sample across the keys: filter, LFO (with tempo sync), glide, mono
mode, **From Selection** to make an instrument from the audio you have selected, a one-page
editor; Trim, Normalize, Reverse and a loop crossfade; **Slice** mode with slice-to-MIDI and the
slices across the keys; **Warp** with the sample's tempo detected.

### Musical Typing

The computer keyboard plays notes (Window › Musical Typing, or **Shift+K** to open it ready to
play). Octave and velocity buttons, a **Sustain** button (Tab holds it), and the window shows
which keys do what.

### Export and import

- **MP3 and Opus export** from Bounce Mix and Bounce Stems, with a bitrate choice.
- **Opus import.**

### Fixes

- A pad's play button is heard with the transport stopped.
- The SM Drums hi-hats were far too quiet: the recorded kits are balanced by loudness, and every
  pad has a Trim knob.
