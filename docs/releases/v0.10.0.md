## What's new in 0.10.0

A large update across the whole program: playing, recording, editing, mixing and MIDI were each
compared against Pro Tools, Studio Pro, Logic and Cubase, and the gaps found were worked through
over four rounds. The comparisons, and what is still missing, are in `docs/daw-parity/`.

Much of this has not yet been used on real sessions with real hardware. Keep a copy of any session
that matters before opening it here, and please report what you find.

### Changes you will notice

- **The menus are reorganised.** A new **Transport** menu holds playing, auditioning, locating,
  looping, skip ranges, varispeed and shuttle, QuickPunch, TrackPunch, record modes, Discard Take and Restart
  Take. The "More" submenus (Options › More, Event › More MIDI and the like) are gone: their items
  sit in named groups in their menus. Ten settings you set once are now in
  **Setup › Preferences...** instead of Options: Pan Law, Default Send Level, Pre-Fader Metering, Pre-Fader Sends
  Ignore Mute, Thin Automation After Pass, Automatic Edge Fades, Suspend Plug-ins on Silence,
  Imported Loops Follow Tempo and Key, Feedback Protection and Edit/Tool Mode Keyboard Lock. The
  Tour of FreeDAWm is in the FreeDAWm menu. Shortcuts are unchanged.
- A greyed-out menu item says why when you hover it, and an item that finds nothing to act on says
  so in the status line.
- **There is no browser version any more.** It is discontinued and removed: FreeDAWm is a desktop
  app.
- **Pairing a PC with the booth for the first time needs 0.10.0 on both.** An older FreeDAWm on
  either side is told to update; there is no fallback to the old pairing. A computer paired before
  this release has to pair again.
- **Arming or disarming a track during a take** now starts or ends that track's take there (not
  while loop recording). With Transport › TrackPunch on, disarming punches the track out instead
  and its take records on.
- **An inactive track's plug-ins are unloaded**, and are not loaded when a session opens. Make the
  track active again and they are made from their saved state.
- **A Script MIDI effect is heard one audio buffer late** when you play live. Bounces and recorded
  effect output are on time.
- **Stop lets reverb and delay tails ring out**, heard and metered. A second Stop cuts them.
- **On a MIDI track showing notes, a drag that starts on a note moves the note**, not the clip.
  Drag the clip by its empty space or name bar.
- **Automation on tracks that follow bars and beats** now moves with the music when you edit the
  tempo.
- **Feedback protection is on:** a monitored input that clips for a whole second is silenced on its
  track. Turn monitoring off and on to bring it back.
- **A pan or send lane that bends now moves smoothly** inside each audio buffer instead of in
  small steps: a slight audible difference. Flat lanes mix exactly as before.
- **AFL and PFL solo** now behave as such. They used to act like an ordinary solo.
- **Automation after a plug-in with latency** now lands on the audio it was written against, so it
  plays later by that latency than before.
- **Mod-wheel vibrato** on the synth and sampler is off until you turn its amount up.
- **New MIDI recordings merge** with what is there by default.
- **SIM** profiles are checked so none is ever above the mix; one headphone profile is 0.6 dB
  quieter than in 0.9.12.

### Playing audio

- Starts, stops, locates and loop wraps no longer click.
- **Warp markers:** a "warp" track view. Click a clip to add a marker, drag it (heard while you
  drag), Alt-click to delete it. Drag a bar line on the Tempo ruler to warp time.
- A tempo change, a stretch or a warp move is heard at once, a little grainy, and the clean render
  takes over when it is ready.
- **Transport › Varispeed...** (optionally keeping pitch) and **Shuttle**. Shuttle Back plays
  through the whole mixer, so a reverb rings backwards as tape does.
- **Transport › Skip Range** jumps playback over the selection. **Dynamic Transport** keeps the
  play start apart from the selection. **Audition** plays up to or from a selection's edges.
- **Setup › Session...** converts the session to another sample rate.
- **File › Open › Session...** opens a session bundle (.fdawmz) by unpacking it into a new
  folder beside it.
- Imported loops that state their tempo and key follow the session's.
- An output device that went away is reconnected when it comes back.
- The audio thread asks for realtime priority; **Window › System Usage** says whether it got it.

### Recording audio

- Record from a bus, or through a track's inserts. Takes as WAV, Broadcast WAV or AIFF.
- **Options › Recording:** Arm Follows Selection, Lock Record (a stray Stop does not end a take),
  Pre-Record (keeps the 10 seconds before Record), Wait for Note and Input Only Monitoring.
- **Record Safe** (right-click a track's R key), **Transport › Restart Take**, and
  **Transport › TrackPunch**: disarm and arm tracks during a take to punch each out and in.
- **Setup › Disk Allocation...** chooses where each track records; the counter shows the
  recording time left on that disk.
- Inputs of up to 16 channels: a 5.1 track records every channel.
- The click can play on its own hardware output (Setup › Click/Countoff...).
- An input device at another rate is converted cleanly, so takes do not alias.

### Editing

- **Options › Overlap:** a clip moved, pasted or recorded onto another overwrites it, layers over
  it or crossfades with it. **Edit groups** apply a move, trim or fade to every grouped track.
- **Clip › Real-Time:** Transpose, Reverse in Place, Loop in Place and Nudge Contents, with no
  new file.
- **Tools:** Scissors and Mute; the Smart Tool draws a crossfade where two clips meet; snap to
  the playhead; a grid that adapts to the zoom.
- **Edit › Selection:** extend to the session's ends, select clips by track, name, colour or
  mute, nudge one edge. Type the selection's start, end or length into the counters.
- **Takes:** Solo Take, comping grouped tracks together, Flatten Comp.
- **Arranger:** right-click a block on the Sections ruler to copy, move, repeat or remove that
  part of the song. **Track › Arrangements** keeps several versions of the song.
- **Folders:** basic folders that only organise; a closed folder edits its tracks as one.
- **Process › Sample Editor...**, a Pencil that redraws samples at the closest zoom, a spectral
  track view, and **Process › Process History...** to bypass or remove an earlier Process.
- **Beat Detective** conforms hits to the grid or a groove, smooths the gaps and writes the hits
  as MIDI notes.
- In the Clip List: drag clips onto tracks, relink a missing file, Compact files to what the clips
  use, and keep clips from **Clip › Capture**.
- Location recordings: a clip's right-click menu › Field Recording expands it to its other
  channels or switches to another take of the scene.
- Clips with automation of their own (Edit › Automation), clip notes, track markers, Lock Track,
  memory locations that recall the view, Edit › Trim › To Close Gaps, tap tempo, Strip Silence
  previews, and audio clips pasted into another session bring their audio.
- A fade dialog that places a crossfade before, across or after the cut, a clip gain line, clip
  groups, box selection, audio quantize, bounce a range in place, and Alt-trim to stretch a clip.
  New fades are equal-power by default.

### Mixing

- **Window › Control Room:** monitor sets with level, Dim, Mono and Mute; a listen bus for AFL
  and PFL; cue mixes on hardware outputs; talkback (hold F11).
- **Window › New Mix Window...** opens more Mix windows. The Mix window gets Find and Spill;
  **Track › Pin** keeps a track in view in both windows.
- Every insert has a wet/dry Mix and can run **post-fader**. **Modulators** (an insert's menu):
  LFOs and envelope followers that move any plug-in parameter.
- **Hardware Insert** (Utilities) puts outboard gear in the chain, with a Ping for its delay.
  **Multi-Mono** (a module's right-click menu) runs one instance per channel, linked or not.
- A bus's strip has a **Summing** menu (Console or Tape), off by default.
- Several outputs from one track (Add Output in its output selector), sends to hardware outputs
  and to tracks, nested VCAs, mix snapshots, track delay, polarity per channel, linked or mirrored
  pans, copying a strip's settings to other tracks, and momentary solo.
- **Automation:** Preview and Punch Preview, Write on Stop, a Touch return time, Show Last
  Touched, lanes for bypass, wet/dry and the surround panner, Pencil shapes, and plug-in
  parameters that change inside an audio buffer. You hear a control as you move it while writing.
- A compressor or gate can be keyed from another track or an aux, in CLAP and VST3 plug-ins too.
- Hosted plug-ins get A/B in their windows; every Presets menu has Copy and Paste Settings;
  plug-in windows can follow the selected track.
- Meter scales including PPM, a Loudness Meter window with loudness range.
- **File › Bounce Mix...** converts the rate, dithers, writes mono or one file per channel, several
  formats at once, tags WAV, MP3, FLAC, AIFF and Opus files, and can add the result to the session; it can bounce a hardware output,
  or play the range in real time through outboard gear. Stems of chosen tracks and busses.
- **File › Import › Session Data...** can bring another session's settings onto tracks of the
  same name.

### Effects and instruments

- **Pantry**, a multisampler: **Track › Capture to Pantry...** records any instrument track,
  through its effects, note by note in velocity layers into a new instrument. Pantry also loads
  SFZ and Decent Sampler libraries and plays its samples from disk. See `docs/pantry.md`.
- **Keel**, a bass amp, and **Heft**, a bass enhancer.
- Meter plug-ins, a Tuner, a Channel Tool, Dither and Lo-Fi, under Utilities.
- **Clip Effects** take a chain of up to eight built-in, CLAP or VST3 effects per clip.
- Comp gets a look-ahead, Dist an 8x oversampling choice, Vault can reverse, trim and stretch its
  impulse response, and CleanQ a Tilt preset.
- The three built-in synths have names and faces: **Burley** (the subtractive synth), **Cicada**
  (a new wavetable synth) and **Steeple** (a new FM synth), under Track › Instrument. Each is drawn
  as an instrument: a printed panel between wood cheeks, its sections framed in signal order, knobs,
  envelope faders, displays and a preset display with previous and next. Sessions that use them
  open as before (the plug-in ids and parameters are unchanged).
- Cicada and Steeple come with twelve factory presets each. Both are made in code and were not
  listened to: the presets are starting points.
- Burley gains filter types, an LFO, sub and noise, unison, sync, mono and legato, and factory
  presets (written without listening: starting points).
- **Plug-ins in their own process:** Setup › Preferences... › Mixing › Run CLAP and VST3 plug-ins
  in their own process (off by default). A crash then silences only that plug-in; its tile says
  "crashed" and Restart Plug-in brings it back.
- Bells and shelves keep their shape near 20 kHz. The sampler no longer aliases when pitched up,
  and a stolen voice fades instead of clicking.

### MIDI

- **MIDI effects** before an instrument (track input menu › MIDI Effects): Arpeggiator, Chord,
  Scale (which can follow the chord lane), Note Repeat and **Script**, which runs a script you
  write (Edit Script... opens the editor, with four examples). A MIDI clip can have its own
  effects too.
- **Score:** enter and edit notes in **Window › Score Editor**; rests, ties, beams and lyrics;
  printed pages, parts and staff size (File › Score Setup...). **File › Import › MusicXML...**,
  and MusicXML export writes key signatures.
- **Step sequences** for any instrument (Clip › New Step Sequence), with value rows that set a
  controller or a track parameter per step, and pattern variations A to H.
- **Window › Chord Pads...**, Event › Write Arpeggio and Write Progression, Event › Tempo from
  MIDI Performance, a global transpose on the Key ruler, drum maps, and a rule-based Select/Split
  Notes that can delete, mute or change matching notes or controller events (MIDI Operations ›
  Select Notes).
- **Layers & Splits** (track output menu): one track plays up to four instruments by key and
  velocity. **Record on the Target** (track output menu › Play Instrument On) puts the take on
  the tracks it played.
- Instruments with several outputs, built-in and hosted, play on mixer channels of their own
  (track output menu › Instrument Outputs).
- Per-note expression and MPE zones, sent to CLAP and VST3 instruments; articulations switched by
  keyswitch; 14-bit and RPN/NRPN controllers edited as one value.
- **SysEx** is recorded, shown in the MIDI Event List and the MIDI Editor's SysEx lane, and played
  to External MIDI, CLAP and VST3.
- **Studio Setup** (Setup › MIDI Input Devices...) names the devices on each MIDI output port, so
  track output menus list them by name and channel.
- **Take lanes** for MIDI. A MIDI track can play an instrument on another track (track output
  menu › Play Instrument On). Notes are edited directly on a MIDI track.
- MIDI output to hardware, MIDI clock and timecode out and followed in, MMC out, Mackie Control
  and HUI surfaces, and MIDI learn.
- Capture what you played while stopped, step input, an input filter and transforms.
- In the editor: several clips at once, smooth controller ramps, a drum view, an event list,
  scales and chords, grooves that keep their accents, per-note probability, shared clip copies
  that change together, and **Clip › Export Clip as MIDI...**

### Vocals, remote recording and live

- **Align timing** in Vocal Studio lines a double's notes up with the lead's, keeping pitch.
- Computers on your network connect to the booth over an encrypted link; the first pairing's code
  proves both computers and is never sent. Talk reaches the remote performer.
- **Window › Set List** plays the session's songs in order, with Go On and a Stage View.

### New third-party components

- `thread-priority` (MIT), for realtime priority.
- `spake2` and `hkdf` (MIT or Apache-2.0), for pairing, with `hmac` (MIT or Apache-2.0) and
  `curve25519-dalek` (BSD-3-Clause).
- `rhai` (MIT or Apache-2.0), for the Script MIDI effect. It brings `smartstring` under MPL-2.0
  and `tiny-keccak` under CC0-1.0.

### Not yet

- A large playback buffer beside a small one for live tracks: every track runs at the audio
  device's buffer size.
- Audio Units cannot go on a clip, take a sidechain key or run in their own process (these need a
  Mac to build and test).
- A plug-in in its own process has no editor window of its own (generic controls instead) and no
  extra outputs, and the setting is for all plug-ins, not chosen per insert.
- A script's parameters cannot be automated, and a clip's own MIDI effects take no script.
- Pantry: no editing of single samples on the shelves and no envelope per part.
- A rack with parallel chains and macro knobs; a chord track that
  re-pitches audio; spectral editing (the view only).
- The surround panner pans mono and stereo tracks only.
- The Set List cannot switch to songs in other sessions.
- Realtime priority on macOS is untested.
- A MIDI device unplugged and plugged back within two seconds is noticed on Linux only.
- ARA editors such as Melodyne and VocAlign: planned for the next release.
- Not planned for now: files at another sample rate in one session (they are converted when
  imported), and several output devices at once.

### How this was checked

The automated tests pass. Nothing in this release was listened to, tried on audio hardware, or run
against third-party plug-ins: plug-in features were tested with the small test plug-ins in the
source tree.

Everything from 0.9.12 (EAR and SIM, the Hearing Test) and earlier is included.
