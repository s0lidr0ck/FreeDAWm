## What's new in 0.10.0

A large update across the whole program: playing, recording, editing, mixing and MIDI were each
compared against Pro Tools, Studio Pro, Logic and Cubase, and the gaps found were worked through.
The comparisons and what is still missing are in `docs/daw-parity/`.

This release has passed its automated tests on Linux, macOS and Windows, but much of it has not
yet been used on real sessions with real hardware. Keep a copy of any session that matters before
opening it here, and please report what you find.

### Playing and editing audio

- Starts, stops, locates and loop wraps no longer click.
- **Warp markers:** a "warp" track view. Click a clip to add a marker, drag it, Alt-click to
  delete it.
- A tempo change, a stretch or a warp move is heard at once, a little grainy, and the clean render
  takes over when it is ready.
- **Options › Overlap:** a clip moved, pasted or recorded onto another overwrites it, layers over
  it or crossfades with it. Send to Back and Bring to Front now work.
- **Edit groups:** a move, trim or fade on one grouped track applies to the others.
- **Clip › Real-Time:** Transpose, Reverse in Place and Loop in Place, with no new file.
- Varispeed that keeps pitch, shuttle forwards and backwards, and scrubbing heard through the
  track's effects and fader, or by dragging on the ruler.
- A fade dialog, a clip gain line, clip groups, box selection, audio quantize, bounce a range in
  place, and Alt-trim to stretch a clip.
- New fades are equal-power by default.

### Recording audio

- Record from a bus, or through a track's inserts.
- Takes as WAV, Broadcast WAV or AIFF.
- QuickPunch, record modes, and Discard Take.

### Mixing

- **Window › Control Room:** monitor sets with level, Dim, Mono and Mute; a listen bus for AFL
  and PFL solo; cue mixes on hardware outputs; talkback (hold F11).
- **Post-fader inserts**, from an insert's menu.
- Sends to hardware outputs and to tracks, nested VCAs, a group dialog, mix snapshots, track
  delay, and width and balance with automation.
- A compressor or gate can be keyed from another track or an aux, including in CLAP plug-ins.
- Meter scales, a Loudness Meter window, and bounce to a loudness target.

### Effects and instruments

- **Keel**, a bass amp, and **Heft**, a bass enhancer.
- Meter plug-ins, a Tuner, a Channel Tool and Dither, under Utilities.
- Bells and shelves keep their shape near 20 kHz.
- The sampler no longer aliases when pitched up, and a stolen voice fades instead of clicking.

### MIDI

- **MIDI effects** before an instrument: Arpeggiator, Chord, Scale and Note Repeat (track input
  menu › MIDI Effects).
- **Take lanes** for MIDI, with the same picking and swiping as audio takes.
- A MIDI track can play an instrument on another track (track output menu › Play Instrument On).
- MIDI output to hardware, MIDI clock and timecode out, Mackie Control and HUI surfaces, and
  MIDI learn.
- Capture what you played while stopped, step input, an input filter and transforms.
- In the editor: several clips at once, smooth controller ramps, a drum view, an event list,
  scales and chords, grooves that keep their accents, per-note probability, and shared clip copies
  that change together.
- **Clip › Export Clip as MIDI…**

### Vocals and remote recording

- **Align timing** in Vocal Studio lines a double's notes up with the lead's, keeping pitch.
- Computers on your network now connect to the booth over an encrypted link, and Talk reaches the
  remote performer.

### What your existing sessions will notice

- **AFL and PFL solo** now behave as such. They used to act like an ordinary solo.
- **Automation after a plug-in with latency** now lands on the audio it was written against, so
  it plays later by that latency than before.
- **Mod-wheel vibrato** on the synth and sampler is off until you turn its amount up.
- **New MIDI recordings merge** with what is there by default.
- **A computer paired with the booth** before this release has to pair again.
- **SIM** profiles are checked so none is ever above the mix; one headphone profile is 0.6 dB
  quieter than in 0.9.12.

### Not yet

- Plug-ins can run in their own process so a crash does not take the session down, but only CLAP
  plug-ins, and only with a developer switch; there is no setting for it yet.
- Instruments with several outputs, score note entry, SysEx recording and real-time bounce.
- Replugging a MIDI device while the app is open is noticed on Linux only.

Everything from 0.9.12 (EAR and SIM, the Hearing Test) and earlier is included.
