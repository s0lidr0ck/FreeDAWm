# Sound Library: candidates to review

Free sampled instruments, mostly in SFZ format, that the owner is auditioning for a downloadable
FreeDAWm Sound Library. Nothing here is bundled yet. Before an instrument goes in, re-read the
license file inside its download (catalog listings can be out of date) and add an
`ATTRIBUTION.md` row.

**How to listen:** put the free **sfizz** (open source) or **Sforzando** (Plogue) VST3 player on
an instrument track, load the library's `.sfz` file, and play it from a keyboard at soft,
medium and hard velocities. Catalog with per-instrument licenses:
<https://sfzinstruments.github.io> (sections: pianos, drums, drum machines, synthesizers, vocals,
and more). Most libraries below are also on GitHub at <https://github.com/sfzinstruments>.

**License key**

- **CC0 / Public domain:** ship freely, credit optional (we credit anyway).
- **CC-BY:** ship with a visible credit to the author.
- **CC-BY-SA:** ship with credit; changes to the samples themselves must stay under the same license.
- **NC (non-commercial):** fine for the owner's own listening, **not** for the library
  (FreeDAWm users make commercial music).

Mark each box when reviewed; note Keep / Maybe / Pass.

## Orchestral, world and everything else

- [ ] **VCSL, Versilian Community Sample Library**: the broadest CC0 set (keys, percussion,
      orchestral, world, oddities). Best single starting point. <https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL>
- [ ] **VSCO 2 Community Edition**, Versilian: chamber orchestra (strings, winds, brass,
      percussion). Widely listed as CC0, but Versilian's site words its terms more narrowly;
      confirm before shipping. <https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE>
- [ ] **Cowsynth**, Karoryfer: transistor organ in organ, pad and pluck versions. CC0.
      <https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.cowsynth>
- [ ] **smpldsnds collection**: many small CC0 and public-domain instruments, including the Sonic Pi
      samples. Skip anything marked "unknown". <https://github.com/smpldsnds>
- [ ] ~~Sonatina Symphonic Orchestra~~: CC Sampling Plus 1.0, too restrictive to ship.

## Gaps

No free, shippable choir or full string section turned up beyond what VSCO 2 CE and VCSL contain.
Worth a second look later. Freesound (<https://freesound.org>, filtered to CC0) is the place for
single hits and textures.
