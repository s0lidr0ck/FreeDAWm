FreeDAWm is a pure-Rust digital audio workstation with a familiar Edit/Mix workflow, aiming to
cover everything a professional studio DAW does, and then go beyond it: faster, open, scriptable
and agent-controllable. Coverage is measured against Pro Tools, the most widely used reference. This page is
the one-page summary; the detail is in [`docs/target-app-parity.md`](docs/target-app-parity.md)
(assessment), [`docs/gaps.md`](docs/gaps.md) (ranked work list), [`docs/roadmap.md`](docs/roadmap.md)
(milestones and beta plan) and [`docs/parity-checklist.md`](docs/parity-checklist.md) (generated
menu checklist).

**Why pre-alpha:** the core-workflow gate in [docs/roadmap.md](docs/roadmap.md#alpha-gate) fails.
Editing, mixing, bouncing, and saving and reopening work end to end. But recording from a chosen
audio interface at a chosen buffer size is built and still waits for the owner's hardware pass
(and macOS verification). A Pro Tools user can't record MIDI from a keyboard (there is
no MIDI device layer). And they can't bring a session in or hand one back (no AAF/OMF/`.ptx`).
Readiness (~40 %) sits right at the alpha bar, but a gate failure keeps the app pre-alpha. The
2026-10-07 estimate ("~65 % overall, 110–150 h to 100 %") counted feature presence. This one
measures readiness and adds hardware, localization and field bug reports.

## Robustness

Property tests drive random edit sequences (with full undo), mutated session files and hostile
command parameters through the engine and mixer; they have caught fade, overflow and
unbounded-allocation bugs. Every command also runs with empty parameters on empty and demo
sessions. Hosted plugins live in isolated unsafe crates (`clap-host`, `vst3-host`, `au-host`) and
are created, loaded and destroyed off the audio thread. The video decoders survive hundreds of
mutated and truncated movies. Field reports from the first public week are tracked in
[gaps.md](docs/gaps.md) (G-04).

## Progress log

- **2026-11-10:** Generate Song (desktop only). AI › Generate Song… (`ml.song`, `ui.ml.song`, `freedawm-cli ml song`) asks YuE2 or ACE-Step 1.5 for a whole song through their MIT C++/GGML servers running locally (yue2.cpp's `yue-server`, acestep.cpp's `ace-server`, same job protocol; not bundled): the song on a new audio track at the edit cursor, and YuE2's score (ABC) as MIDI tracks per voice plus a chords track, one undo step; Cancel cancels the server's job. A new ABC reader (`abc`: keys and modes, accidentals, ties, tuplets, chords, chord symbols) turns YuE2's scores into notes. Same style and lyrics into both for comparing. No readiness percentage change.
- **2026-11-10:** Generate MIDI from Text (desktop only). AI › Generate MIDI from Text… (`ml.text_midi`, `ui.ml.text_midi`, `freedawm-cli ml text-midi`) runs MIDI-LLM (Llama 3.2 1B with the Anticipatory Music Transformer's MIDI tokens) on a prompt and puts one instrument track per General MIDI instrument at the edit cursor (Drum Synth on channel 10, Subtractive Synth with a program change elsewhere, each instrument on its own channel for MIDI export), one undo step; length, randomness, a repeatable seed, and Continue from a selected MIDI clip (`continue_from`, `--continue IN.mid`: its notes start the piece and the model carries on). New model `midi_llm` (8-bit `MatMulNBits` on ONNX Runtime, 2.0 GB; top-1 agreement with the PyTorch original 97 %), a pure-Rust Llama 3 tokenizer (`llama_bpe`, token-exact against `tokenizers`), golden tests. No readiness percentage change.
- **2026-11-06:** Lift - Sampler, steps 2–4. Cleanup: `mix.sampler_edit` trims silence (below -60 dB of the peak), normalizes to -0.3 dBFS or reverses the sample as a new source (one undo step; the start, end, loop and slice markers stay on the same audio), and a Loop Crossfade (equal power, up to half a forward loop) smooths the seam. Slice mode: Play › Slice plays slice N on the root key + N at the sample's own pitch, from its start to the next slice's; slices (up to 64) are parameters (`slice_count`, `slice_1`…`slice_64`, group Slices), cut by `mix.sampler_slice` at the hits (`soundcraft_dsp::slices::hits`, with a sensitivity), on a beat grid at the sample's tempo, or into equal parts; `mix.sampler_slice_midi` writes a MIDI clip that plays them in their order and timing, to rearrange; `mix.sampler_slice_play` plays one slice across the keyboard. Warp: the sample plays at the song's tempo over its own (`sample_bpm`) with its pitch from the keys, by two Hann grains aligned by cross-correlation (WSOLA-style); `mix.sampler_detect` reports the sample's tempo (beat tracking) and Lift offers Use tempo. The LFO syncs to the song (1/32 to 2 bars, triplets), its phase locked to the transport. Lift shows it all: Trim / Normalize / Reverse and the crossfade under the waveform; numbered slice lines (click to pick, drag to move), Hits / Beats / Equal, To MIDI and Slice → Keys; the Warp row with the sample tempo; LFO Sync with a Division knob. No readiness percentage change.
- **2026-11-06:** Lift - Sampler. The built-in Sampler (id `sampler`, unchanged) is renamed Lift and grows into a playable instrument. Sound: a Sample End point (start and end swap when crossed), a resonant filter (Off, low-, high- or band-pass, the band-pass at unity peak) with its own Amount/Attack/Decay envelope, an LFO (sine, triangle, square, saw, random; 0.05–20 Hz) to pitch, filter and volume, glide, Poly or Mono (legato, back to the key still held) voicing and pan; the new parameters default to off, so older sessions sound the same. Sampling from the session: `mix.sampler_from_selection` renders the selected range of an audio track (as its clips play) into the sample. `mix.sampler_detect` finds the pitch a sample sounds at (YIN over its louder windows; none for drums and noise). The instrument window is a one-page editor in the Juke family's look (walnut ground, tobacco cards, cream knobs with amber value rings, curves in dark wells with cream handles; `crates/ui-egui/src/lift.rs`): the sample with Load Sample…, From Selection and Clear, the detected root note with Use as Root, a 150-point waveform with S/E and loop markers that drag (unplayed parts dimmed), Loop/Play/Voices switches, knob panels for Pitch, Filter (with its response curve), Amp Envelope (a shape whose points drag), LFO and Output, and a keyboard showing the key range (edges drag) and root (double-click a key). `ui.mix.instrument_window` opens it from scripts. Tempo detection and LFO tempo sync wait for Warp. No readiness percentage change.
